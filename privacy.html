Privacy

Your playing stays yours.

Strategic Fretboard does not collect personal data from the iPhone or iPad app. Your instrument, tuning, root note, scale, chord, orientation, and display preferences are stored locally on your device so the app can restore your setup.

Microphone access

The automatic tuner uses microphone input only while you use the tuner. Audio is analyzed on your device and is not recorded, uploaded, retained, or shared.

Website

This promotional website does not require an account or payment information. Standard hosting infrastructure may process limited technical information necessary to deliver the page securely.

Questions

For privacy or support questions, use the developer contact provided on the Strategic Fretboard App Store listing.
